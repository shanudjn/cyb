How to use the full material after this week

First 30 days

Work through the 6.858 lecture sequence and pair each topic with one local lab and a one-page explanation in your own words.

Days 31–60

Use 6.857 problem sets to deepen cryptographic and network-security reasoning. Explain every solution; do not memorize it.

Days 61–90

Complete an original capstone: threat model, implementation, security tests, detection requirements and a professional report.

Safety rule

MIT’s own security syllabi emphasize legal and ethical limits. Keep all attack work inside your lab or a written authorization boundary.
